The Molecule

Arabiogalactan

A branched polysaccharide from Siberian larch. More than a carrier — AG amplifies DHQ bioavailability by ×2.8, acts as a prebiotic, and modulates immune response through its own independent pathways.

From larch to
precision polysaccharide

Arabiogalactan is extracted from the heartwood and sapwood of Larix gmelinii — the same Dahurian Larch that yields DHQ. The two molecules coexist in the same tree, extracted in a single integrated process, then co-processed to maximize their synergistic effect.

MW 10,000–120,000 Da · β-1,3-galactan backbone · L-arabinose side chains
×2.8
DHQ bioavailability when combined with AG vs. DHQ alone
Properties

Arabiogalactan profile

Prebiotic Fiber (FDA)
Recognized prebiotic. Selectively fermented by Bifidobacterium and Lactobacillus. Increases short-chain fatty acid production.
Immune Modulation
Stimulates NK-cell and macrophage activity via Toll-like receptor 4 (TLR4). Documented in J. Immunol. (2000).
Highly Water-Soluble
Dissolves freely in water at high concentration. No emulsifier needed. Compatible with beverages and liquid formats.
Heat Stable
Retains structure through pasteurization, UHT, and baking conditions. No activity loss at processing temperatures.
Neutral Taste
Slightly sweet, no off-notes. Does not dominate flavor profiles. Suitable for gummies, drinks, and tablets alike.
Excellent Excipient
Used as a natural binder, filler, and film-former. Reduces tablet friability and improves capsule filling uniformity.
