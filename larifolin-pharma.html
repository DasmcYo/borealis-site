Pharmaceutical precision.
Every batch.

Pharmaceutical and OTC applications require a level of analytical documentation that goes beyond standard supplement-grade ingredients. Larifolin Pharma is formulated and documented to meet that standard — per-batch HPLC verification, full pharmacokinetic profile, GHS-compliant safety data, and jurisdiction-specific regulatory guidance.

DHQ is the primary pharmacologically active molecule: documented mechanisms of action across four pathways (antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, cardioprotective, neuroprotective), known metabolic route via CYP3A4, confirmed BBB penetration, and established safety profile with LD50 >2 g/kg.

AG acts as a bioavailability enhancer and pharmaceutical excipient with its own documented prebiotic and immune-modulating activity. Both molecules are included in a combined analytical package with separate and combined assay data.

Benefits

Clinical-grade.
Documented claims.

99%+ verified purity
Every batch undergoes HPLC analysis with third-party verification. CoA issued per lot. Identity testing via UV/Vis and mass spectrometry available on request.
Pharmacokinetics documented
Half-life ~4h, CYP3A4 metabolic pathway, water solubility ~1.5 g/L. No key enzyme inhibition at therapeutic doses. Full PK data sheet available with partnership agreement.
Blood-brain barrier penetration
DHQ crosses the BBB — confirmed in murine model (PNAS 2019). Detected in brain tissue and cerebrospinal fluid. Relevant for OTC neuroprotective and cognitive health applications.
Established safety record
LD50 >2000 mg/kg (murine). NOAEL 500 mg/kg/day. Russian Pharmacopoeia registered since 1990s as Diquertin. No genotoxicity or mutagenicity in standard assays.
Regulatory dossier support
We provide jurisdiction-specific documentation: EU Novel Food EFSA dossier support, US NDI/GRAS package, Russian Pharmacopoeia reference, EAEU food additive file. Country-by-country advisory included.
GMP manufacturing
Produced under GMP conditions. ISO 22000, HACCP certified. Full chain-of-custody from Siberian larch forest to finished ingredient. Batch traceability to source lot.
