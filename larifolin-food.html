Formula Rationale

Antioxidant enrichment that
survives processing.

Most polyphenols degrade at food-processing temperatures. DHQ is a thermal exception: documented stability to 150°C in neutral to mildly acidic environments. This makes it compatible with hot-fill beverages, pasteurised dairy, baked goods, and extruded products without degradation of antioxidant activity.

Water solubility (~1.5 g/L) removes the need for an oil carrier or emulsifier — dramatically simplifying functional beverage and liquid supplement formulations. AG contributes natural fibre content (CODEX-compliant) and a neutral organoleptic profile, acting as a clean-label carrier.

No E-number classification required for AG at standard functional food inclusion rates. Natural origin, Siberian-sourced, non-GMO — fully compatible with clean label and organic-adjacent positioning.

Benefits

Functional enrichment
without compromise.

Thermal stability
Stable to 150°C in standard food matrices. Retains antioxidant activity after hot-fill processing, pasteurisation at 85°C, UHT at 135°C, and baking cycles.
Water-soluble
~1.5 g/L solubility in water — 200× higher than quercetin. Direct dissolution in aqueous beverage and dairy matrices without emulsifier, oil carrier, or pH adjustment.
Clean label
Natural origin, non-GMO, Siberian-sourced. AG classified as natural polysaccharide (not E-numbered). No synthetic additives in the ingredient. Meets clean label standards in EU and USA markets.
Prebiotic fibre content
AG contributes CODEX-compliant dietary fibre. Enables fibre and prebiotic claims on functional food products. Documented selective stimulation of Bifidobacterium and Lactobacillus strains.
Neutral organoleptic
No detectable taste, colour, or off-note at standard functional food inclusion rates. Slight mildly sweet note from AG at higher concentrations — aids palatability without masking agents.
Antioxidant label claim
Documented ORAC activity enables antioxidant claims in markets where ORAC-based positioning is permitted. Supports "contains antioxidants" and "protects cells from oxidative stress" claim categories.
