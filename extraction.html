Siberia

From tree to molecule

Ethanol extraction from Dahurian Larch sapwood. Chromatographic purification to ≥99% purity (HPLC-UV). Full quality control from raw material to final product — no solvents, no compromise.

01
Raw Material
Sawmill residue collection
Sapwood and heartwood chips are sourced from certified Siberian larch timber mills in Yakutia and Irkutsk Oblast. No living trees are felled. Raw material moisture content and DHQ concentration are tested at intake.
02
Primary Extraction
Aqueous-ethanol extraction
Hot aqueous-ethanol extraction at controlled temperature (40–60°C). Ethanol concentration optimized per batch to maximize DHQ yield while minimizing co-extractables. Solvent fully recovered and recycled — zero waste ethanol disposal.
03
Purification
Chromatographic separation
Column chromatography on macroporous resin. DHQ and AG are separated in this stage. Each fraction undergoes HPLC-UV identity and purity check. Only fractions meeting ≥99% purity threshold proceed to the next stage.
04
QC / Final Product
Drying · testing · packaging
Spray-drying or crystallization depending on target particle size. Final product testing: HPLC purity, heavy metals (ICP-MS), microbiology (USP <61>), residual solvents (ICH Q3C). Certificate of Analysis issued per lot. Packaged under nitrogen to prevent oxidation.
