Formula Rationale

The only flavonoid with
confirmed BBB crossing.

The longevity supplement market is crowded with antioxidants that never reach the brain. Most polyphenols are stopped at the blood-brain barrier (BBB) — the brain's tightest checkpoint. DHQ is an exception documented in peer-reviewed literature: a 2019 PNAS study confirmed that DHQ crosses the BBB and exhibits neuroprotective activity in vivo.

This makes DHQ structurally unique in the flavonoid class for longevity formulations targeting cognitive reserve, neural inflammation, and age-related oxidative stress accumulation in CNS tissue.

AG addresses the longevity-microbiome axis: prebiotic modulation of Bifidobacterium and Lactobacillus populations supports SCFA production, reduced systemic inflammation, and optimised gut-brain signalling — pathways directly implicated in healthy cognitive ageing. The two molecules operate at complementary layers of the longevity stack.

Evidence-backed longevity pathways

Prebiotic effect · documented microbiome modulation
01
Neuroprotection via BBB penetration
DHQ crosses the blood-brain barrier and accumulates in CNS tissue. Once inside, it scavenges reactive oxygen species in neural cells, suppresses neuroinflammation, and protects mitochondrial integrity in neurons — mechanisms directly relevant to age-related cognitive decline.
02
Cellular antioxidant defence
Systemic ORAC activity — ROS neutralisation in peripheral tissue and the circulation. Protects lipid membranes from peroxidation, preserves endothelial function, reduces markers of oxidative biological ageing.
03
Microbiome optimisation via AG
AG selectively stimulates Bifidobacterium and Lactobacillus, increasing SCFA output (butyrate, propionate). These metabolites reduce systemic low-grade inflammation (inflammaging) — a primary driver of biological age acceleration.
04
Gut-brain axis support
SCFA produced by AG-stimulated microbiota signal via the vagal nerve and bloodstream to modulate brain immune activity. Optimal gut-brain axis function is associated with maintained cognitive reserve and reduced neuro-inflammatory load with age.
