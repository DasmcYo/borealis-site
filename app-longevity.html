Application 05

Longevity
& Anti-aging

Targeting the cellular mechanisms of aging — not just the symptoms.

Nutraceuticals Sports Nutrition Food & Beverage Pharmaceuticals Longevity

The challenge:
longevity claims require mechanistic credibility.

The longevity market is expanding — but regulatory scrutiny is rising with it. Generalist antioxidant claims are increasingly insufficient. Buyers in this sector — whether B2B formulators or informed end consumers — expect specificity: which pathways, which markers, which evidence.

Chronic low-grade inflammation (inflammaging) is now recognised as a central driver of biological aging across multiple organ systems. Any longevity-positioned ingredient that can credibly address inflammatory signalling, oxidative burden, and neurological resilience simultaneously holds a structural advantage in formulation.

Why Larifolin
belongs here.

DHQ has over 70 peer-reviewed publications across cardiovascular, neuroprotective, anti-inflammatory, and metabolic research. It is not an emerging ingredient — it is an under-positioned one. The AG matrix that constitutes Larifolin solves two additional longevity-relevant problems: gut microbiome support (prebiotic fibre) and bioavailability enhancement that makes the delivered dose clinically meaningful.

Together, DHQ+AG in Larifolin ratios addresses inflammaging at multiple levels — systemic oxidative stress, gut-brain axis signalling, and neurological cellular protection — within a single ingredient.

Biological Pathways

The mechanisms that matter
in healthy aging

Pathway 01
Inflammaging

DHQ modulates pro-inflammatory cytokine signalling. Chronic systemic inflammation is the primary driver of age-related cellular deterioration across cardiovascular, metabolic, and neurological systems.

Pathway 02
Cellular Oxidative Stress

DHQ is a potent free radical scavenger. Cumulative oxidative damage — lipid peroxidation, protein oxidation, DNA strand breaks — is the molecular substrate of aging. Antioxidant capacity is a measurable, documentable longevity marker.

Pathway 03
Neuroprotection

DHQ crosses the blood-brain barrier in preclinical models. Cognitive decline and neurodegenerative processes are among the most commercially significant aging concerns. BBB-crossing antioxidants are relatively rare in the ingredient space.

Pathway 04
Gut-Brain Axis

AG is a prebiotic dietary fibre that supports gut microbiome diversity. The gut-brain connection is an active area of longevity research — microbiome composition correlates with systemic inflammation, mood, and cognitive function.
