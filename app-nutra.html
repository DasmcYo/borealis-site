Application 01

Nutraceuticals
& Supplements

A bioavailability matrix that makes every active in your formula work harder.

Nutraceuticals Sports Nutrition Food & Beverage Pharmaceuticals Longevity

The challenge:
most actives are poorly absorbed.

The dietary supplement industry has an absorption problem. Vitamins, minerals, botanical extracts, collagen peptides — the majority of them are consumed at doses far higher than necessary, because bioavailability is low and inconsistent. Manufacturers compensate by loading more ingredient per serving. Consumers pay for what their gut discards.

The result is formulations that are expensive to produce, difficult to differentiate, and increasingly questioned by a more science-literate consumer base.

How Biofolin
addresses it.

Arabinogalactan forms a polysaccharide matrix in the gastrointestinal tract that slows the transit of co-formulated actives, protects them from first-pass hepatic clearance, and feeds the gut microbiota that further activate absorbed compounds. The practical effect: the same active, at the same dose, reaches systemic circulation in greater quantity.

DHQ contributes direct antioxidant and anti-inflammatory activity — adding functional value beyond the carrier role. AG adds prebiotic fibre, allowing a gut health claim alongside the primary active.

Key Benefits

What Biofolin adds
to your supplement formula

Enhanced Bioavailability

AG's polysaccharide matrix increases the systemic uptake of co-formulated actives. Measured at ×2.8 over DHQ alone. The same mechanism applies to other ingredients in the blend.

Antioxidant Protection

DHQ is one of the most potent natural flavonoid antioxidants — ORAC 28,000 μmol TE/100g. It protects sensitive actives (omega-3, vitamin A, CoQ10) from oxidative degradation both in the formula and in the body.

Prebiotic Fibre Value

Arabinogalactan is selectively fermented by Bifidobacterium and Lactobacillus. This allows a gut health or prebiotic claim alongside any primary active — expanding the formula's positioning without adding a separate ingredient.
