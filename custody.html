Traceability · Certifications · Lot Tracking

Chain of Custody

Every kilogram of DHQ and AG shipped by Borealis is fully traceable — from the specific forest origin in Yakutia to the analytical certificate accompanying your shipment.

Traceability Chain

From tree to your facility

Origin
Certified forest origin
Raw material (larch sawmill chips) sourced from documented mills in Yakutia and Irkutsk Oblast. Forest region, mill ID, and harvest date recorded per lot.
Production
GMP batch record
Each production batch has a unique lot number. Full batch manufacturing record retained: raw material lot → extraction parameters → purification results → final product QC.
QC Release
Certificate of Analysis per lot
HPLC purity ≥99%, heavy metals (Pb, Cd, As, Hg) by ICP-MS, microbiology (TAMC, TYMC), residual solvents (ICH Q3C). No lot ships without passing release testing.
Packaging
Nitrogen-flushed, sealed containers
Packed under nitrogen atmosphere in food-grade HDPE drums or double-layered PE bags. Lot number and expiry date labeled. Desiccant included. Cold chain documentation for temperature-sensitive orders.
Delivery
Full documentation package
Every shipment includes: Certificate of Analysis, Technical Data Sheet, Safety Data Sheet, Certificate of Origin, and MSDS. Available in English, Russian, and on request for other EU languages.
Certifications

Quality standards

GMP Certified Facility
Production under current Good Manufacturing Practice. Third-party audit performed annually.
ISO 9001:2015
Quality management system certification covering production, QC, and logistics processes.
HACCP Compliant
Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points compliance. Food-grade production environment.
CoA Per Lot
HPLC purity, ICP-MS metals, microbiological testing. No exceptions — every shipment, every time.
