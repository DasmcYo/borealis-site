DHQ demonstrates blood-brain barrier penetration and neuroprotective activity in a murine model of neuroinflammation

DHQ crossed the blood-brain barrier and showed measurable neuroprotective effects in the test model. This study is foundational for pharmaceutical and longevity positioning of Larifolin in neurological applications.

Bioavailability · T-COG Trial

Arabinogalactan as a bioavailability matrix: pharmacokinetic study of DHQ+AG vs. standalone DHQ

Independent clinical pharmacokinetics research

Bioavailability of DHQ was measured at ×2.8 higher in the AG co-administration group vs. standalone DHQ. The effect is attributed to AG's role as a microbiome-mediated carrier that extends intestinal absorption time.

Significance: foundational for all Larifolin bioavailability claims

Significance: antioxidant mechanism robustly documented

Arabinogalactan selectively stimulates Lactobacillus and Bifidobacterium growth in fermentation models

Dietary fibre research, multiple independent studies

AG acts as a prebiotic substrate, selectively feeding beneficial bacterial populations. Relevance: the same AG matrix that enhances DHQ bioavailability also provides independent gut health benefits, enabling dual-claim positioning.

Significance: prebiotic claim supported; dual benefit in one ingredient
Mechanisms

How Larifolin
works at the molecular level

Free Radical Scavenging

DHQ's polyphenolic structure donates hydrogen atoms to neutralise reactive oxygen species. Documented effect across in vitro and in vivo oxidative stress models.

Anti-inflammatory Signalling

DHQ modulates NF-κB and COX-2 pathways — two central mediators of chronic inflammation. Relevant to longevity, recovery, and inflammatory condition management.

Microbiome Enhancement

AG is fermented by colonic bacteria, selectively promoting Lactobacillus and Bifidobacterium. Drives the bioavailability effect and provides independent prebiotic value.

BBB Penetration

DHQ has demonstrated blood-brain barrier crossing in preclinical models. Mechanism linked to lipophilic character enabling passive diffusion through the endothelial cell layer.

Bioavailability Matrix

AG extends intestinal transit time for DHQ, providing a prolonged absorption window. Result: ×2.8 bioavailability in pharmacokinetic studies — the core rationale for the Larifolin combination.

Endothelial Protection

DHQ demonstrates lipid peroxidation inhibition and endothelial membrane protection — relevant to cardiovascular health claims and supplement positioning in heart health categories.
