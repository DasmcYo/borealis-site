Mechanism of synergy

How AG modifies DHQ pharmacokinetics

The ×2.8 increase is not additive — it is structural. Arabiogalactan changes how DHQ behaves in the GI tract at three distinct stages.

01
GI Matrix formation
Protective encapsulation
AG's branched polysaccharide structure forms a gel-like matrix around DHQ in the GI tract. This shields the molecule from enzymatic degradation in the stomach and upper intestine — the primary site of polyphenol loss.
02
First-pass hepatic metabolism ↓
Reduced liver degradation
DHQ absorbed alone undergoes significant first-pass metabolism in the liver (CYP3A4 pathway). AG delays and modulates this absorption, reducing the fraction metabolised before reaching systemic circulation.
03
Gut microbiota modulation
Extended absorption window
AG acts as a prebiotic selectively fermented by Bifidobacterium and Lactobacillus. This shifts the colonic microbiome toward strains that enhance polyphenol bioconversion — extending the effective absorption window beyond the small intestine. Source: Food Chemistry 2021.
