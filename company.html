About BIOLAR

One ingredient.
Precision-engineered.

BIOLAR develops and manufactures Larifolin — a precision DHQ+Arabinogalactan ingredient sourced exclusively from Siberian Dahurian larch. Our focus is narrow by design: one ingredient, five application sectors, a single mission to make the DHQ+AG combination the global reference standard in high-bioavailability botanical actives.

Mission

Why we exist:

DHQ was underdelivering its own potential.

Dihydroquercetin has over 70 peer-reviewed publications and a strong biological rationale. It was being sold as a commodity extract — variable purity, no carrier system, buyers left to figure out formulation themselves.

We built Larifolin to solve the delivery problem. The Arabinogalactan matrix is not an additive — it is the reason the DHQ works at meaningful dose levels. ×2.8 bioavailability is not a marketing claim; it is a pharmacokinetic measurement. That is what we manufacture and supply.

What we actually sell:

We sell a precision ingredient with five calibrated ratio variants, matched to application sector requirements. We do not sell finished products, make health claims to consumers, or retail direct-to-consumer.

Our customers are manufacturers, formulators, and brand owners in nutraceuticals, sports nutrition, functional food, pharmaceutical, and longevity verticals. Every engagement starts with a technical brief and ends with documented specifications — not a catalogue purchase.

Origin

The source of
Larifolin

Siberia · Dahurian Larch

A single species,
a controlled source.

Larix gmelinii — Dahurian larch — is the primary commercial source of DHQ and Arabinogalactan globally. Siberia holds the world's largest natural stands. Our extraction is sourced from the same origin, batch-tested, and documented end to end. No blended origins, no undisclosed substitutions.

Principles

How we work

Documentation First

Every claim we make — bioavailability, purity, stability — is backed by documentation we can share. CoA per batch, technical specs, supporting research. If we can't document it, we don't claim it.

Narrow Focus

We make one ingredient. Not a catalogue of extracts, not a portfolio play. This is not a positioning decision — it is an operational one. Depth beats breadth when precision matters.

B2B Only

We work exclusively with manufacturers, formulators, and brand owners. No DTC, no retail, no consumer marketing. Our sales process is a technical dialogue, not a catalogue transaction.

Timeline

BIOLAR at a
glance

Origin

Biolar Organics — consumer health product company

BIOLAR begins as a consumer health brand developing and selling finished dietary supplements on Russian e-commerce marketplaces. This phase builds deep operational knowledge of the supplement industry — regulatory requirements, formulation constraints, consumer demand patterns.

Pivot

Identification of the Larifolin opportunity

Partner research surfaces the DHQ+Arabinogalactan bioavailability data. The decision: stop building finished products and develop the precision ingredient that finished product companies should be using. Larifolin is conceived as a branded ingredient on the Pycnogenol model.

Now

Global B2B — five application sectors

Larifolin is positioned across nutraceuticals, sports nutrition, functional food, pharmaceuticals, and longevity. The focus is international — EU, Asia-Pacific, Brazil. The Russian market is deliberately excluded from the initial commercial strategy. Five ratio variants calibrated per sector. Samples and documentation available on request.
