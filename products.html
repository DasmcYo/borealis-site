The Foundation

Same source.
Different formula.

"Every Larifolin product starts with the same molecule — from the same Daurian larch, the same Siberian forest."

DHQ (Dihydroquercetin) and AG (Arabinogalactan) are extracted from a single source: Larix gmelinii from the Sakha Republic, 62°N latitude. The extraction process is identical. The purity target is 99%+ for every product in the line.

What differs is the ratio. Each Larifolin formulation adjusts the DHQ:AG balance for its target sector — optimising antioxidant intensity, bioavailability, thermal stability, or neuroprotective depth depending on your application.

One supplier. One supply chain. One quality standard — across all five of your formulation needs.
