Siberia
Larix gmelinii · Eastern Siberia · Permafrost

The tree that
does not die.

800 years in conditions of −60°C. Frozen ground. Six months of polar night. Dahurian Larch survived — and accumulated the molecule of survival.

Source Uniqueness

Where strength grows

🌲
800
Years of life
Dahurian Larch lives up to 800 years in permafrost conditions — the record among Siberian conifers. Extreme longevity drives DHQ accumulation in the heartwood.
🌡️
-60°C
Minimum temperature
Withstands Yakutian winters down to −60°C. Extreme cold stress is the driving force behind DHQ biosynthesis — the tree produces it as cryoprotection.
🗺️
7M km²
Range in Siberia
The largest forested massif on Earth. Larix gmelinii covers most of Eastern Siberia and the Russian Far East — the largest contiguous larch forest.
⚗️
99%
DHQ purity (HPLC)
Cryo-extraction without solvents. Quality control at every step from tree to capsule. Each lot comes with a full Certificate of Analysis.
🔒
Monopoly
On the world market
Dahurian Larch is the only commercially significant source of DHQ in the world. No synthetic pathway exists at commercial scale. There is no alternative.
♻️
Waste
From timber processing
DHQ is extracted from sawmill residues — no living trees are felled for extraction. Fully circular, zero additional deforestation footprint.
