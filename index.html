The Ingredient

Biofolin is a calibrated blend of Dihydroquercetin (DHQ) and Arabinogalactan (AG) — two molecules that interact to produce outcomes neither achieves independently. DHQ contributes potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory activity; AG acts as a bioavailability matrix, extending half-life and modulating gut absorption. The ratio between them is tuned to each application sector.

The Source

DHQ is extracted from Daurian larch (Larix gmelinii) growing in Eastern Siberia — the only commercially viable source of pharmaceutical-grade DHQ at scale.
