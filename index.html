The Molecule

DHQ and Arabiogalactan are not interchangeable antioxidants — they are a studied two-molecule system. DHQ carries one of the strongest ORAC scores in natural chemistry and has confirmed blood-brain barrier penetration. AG acts as a bioavailability vector: extending DHQ half-life, reducing first-pass metabolism, and modulating gut microbiota through mechanisms DHQ alone cannot trigger.
