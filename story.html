About Borealis · Eastern Siberia · Est. 2019

We didn't discover
DHQ. We unlocked it.

A molecule hiding in Siberian larch for 120 million years. A team that spent years building the supply chain to bring it to the world's nutraceutical brands.

Chapter One

It started with a question

"Why is the world's most potent natural antioxidant still unknown to most supplement brands?"

In 2019, a group of researchers and entrepreneurs in Siberia asked a simple question: the dahurian larch tree had been producing DHQ (Dihydroquercetin) for millions of years as a survival mechanism against extreme cold and stress — yet the global nutraceutical market had barely noticed.

The answer wasn't scientific. It was logistical. Extracting DHQ at commercial purity, maintaining a documented supply chain, and bridging the gap between Siberian forest and European or American production lines required infrastructure that simply didn't exist.

So we built it.

Our Timeline
