Five Principles

The beliefs that guide us

01
Science before claims
We don't write a benefit until it's backed by peer-reviewed research. Every claim on this site links to its primary source. If the data is uncertain, we say so explicitly — not in fine print, but in the headline.
02
Radical transparency
Our CoA is public. Our extraction coordinates are on the product page. Our regulatory status per market is documented. We compete on quality, not information asymmetry. If you can verify it yourself — you should.
03
Long relationships over fast deals
We don't do spot sales. Every partner gets full technical support, formulation guidance, and regulatory documentation. Building a great product takes time — we're built for that timeline, not the quarterly one.
04
Zero living tree extraction
DHQ is extracted exclusively from the wood waste of existing timber processing operations. No live larch is harvested for ingredient production. The forest grows while we work — that's not marketing copy, it's supply chain design.
05
Precision over volume
We're not trying to be the biggest DHQ supplier. We're trying to be the most accurate. Every batch is documented. Every shipment has full traceability. We'd rather supply 50 rigorous partners than 500 anonymous ones.

Our Position

What we won't compromise on

"An ingredient company that doesn't publish its CoA is asking you to trust marketing over chemistry. We find that unacceptable — for our partners, and for end consumers."

The supplement industry has a documentation problem. Ingredients are sourced from opaque supply chains, tested internally with no independent verification, and marketed with claims that range from optimistic to misleading.

We built BIOLAR to be the opposite of that. Every batch we ship includes third-party HPLC analysis. Every partnership starts with a full technical dossier, not a sales deck. Every claim we make cites the study it comes from.

This makes us slower to close deals. It means fewer partners, but better ones. It means our partners can put "clinically studied" on their labels and actually mean it.

We believe that's the only sustainable model for a premium ingredient supplier — and the only way to build something that lasts.
