Siberia
Geography · Monopoly · Discovery Timeline

62°N · 130°E
Geography of exclusivity

Eastern Siberia — Yakutia, Buryatia, Irkutsk Oblast — is the only region on Earth where commercial DHQ from Dahurian Larch is produced. There is no alternative source.

Discovery Timeline

From tree to science

~120,000,000 years
Emergence of genus Larix
Larch forests appeared in the Cretaceous period. Since then, the DHQ molecule has evolved as a stress-adaptation system for the tree.
1940s
First Soviet research
Soviet phytochemists isolated DHQ from Larix dahurica. First data on antioxidant activity. The molecule remained largely unknown outside the USSR.
1990s–2000s
Industrialization of production
First industrial DHQ production from larch timber waste in Krasnoyarsk and Irkutsk. Russian pharmacopoeia inclusion as API "Diquertin."
2019
PNAS breakthrough
Publication in PNAS on neuroprotective properties of DHQ brought the molecule to international attention. Blood-brain barrier crossing confirmed for the first time.
2025 — Active
Human RCT in Japan
First randomized controlled human trial — T-COG study, National Center of Neurology and Psychiatry, Japan. Results expected 2026.
