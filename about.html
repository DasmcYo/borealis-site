About Biofolin

Two molecules.
One system.

Biofolin is a precision-ratio blend of DHQ and Arabinogalactan — engineered to work as a single system, calibrated per application sector.

What is Biofolin

A calibrated powder blend of Dihydroquercetin and Arabinogalactan. Not two ingredients mixed. One system, engineered.

Most ingredient suppliers sell DHQ and Arabinogalactan as separate commodities. Biofolin is different: the two molecules are combined at a precision ratio, selected for each application sector, so they function as a single system from the moment they enter a formulation.

DHQ (Dihydroquercetin, also known as Taxifolin) is one of the most potent natural flavonoid antioxidants — with confirmed blood-brain barrier penetration, anti-inflammatory action, and hepatoprotective properties. Arabinogalactan (AG) is a high-molecular-weight polysaccharide that acts as a bioavailability matrix: it extends DHQ's half-life, reduces first-pass metabolism, and feeds the beneficial gut microbiota that further activate the absorbed compound.

The result is not additive. It is synergistic — measured at ×2.8 over DHQ alone.

How It Works

Four mechanisms.
One interaction.

01

Antioxidant & Anti-inflammatory

DHQ neutralises reactive oxygen species with one of the highest ORAC values in natural chemistry — 28,000 μmol TE/100g. It suppresses NF-κB and COX-2 inflammatory pathways without interfering with normal cellular signalling.

02

Bioavailability Matrix

AG forms a polysaccharide matrix in the gut that slows DHQ's transit, reduces first-pass hepatic metabolism, and extends its plasma half-life. The same mechanism enhances absorption of other actives co-formulated with Biofolin.

03

Prebiotic Action

AG is a dietary fibre selectively fermented by Bifidobacterium and Lactobacillus. This produces short-chain fatty acids that improve gut barrier integrity and amplify systemic absorption — creating a gut environment that makes the formula work harder.

04

Blood-Brain Barrier Crossing

Confirmed by a 2019 PNAS paper — DHQ crosses the blood-brain barrier via active transport. In an Alzheimer's mouse model, it reduced amyloid-β plaque load and restored cerebral blood flow. The only flavonoid with this peer-reviewed confirmation at this level.

Provenance

Siberian Dahurian Larch.
The only source.

Biofolin's DHQ is extracted exclusively from Larix gmelinii — Dahurian larch — growing in Eastern Siberia at 62°N 130°E. This species is the sole commercially viable source of pharmaceutical-grade DHQ at scale. No other tree, plant, or region produces it in the concentration or volume required for industrial ingredient supply.

We hold managed extraction rights with full chain-of-custody documentation from standing tree to exported powder. The forest is managed under regeneration protocols. No substitution. No spot sourcing.
