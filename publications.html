One of the three most prestigious scientific journals globally. Taxifolin shown to inhibit amyloid-β aggregation, restore cerebral blood flow, reduce neuroinflammation. In vivo mouse model of Alzheimer's disease.

The world's only active randomized clinical trial on taxifolin in humans. Conducted by Japan's National Center of Brain and Stroke. Phase II. Target outcome: cognitive function in MCI. Results expected 2025–2026.

Arabinogalactan enhances bioavailability of polyphenols via gut microbiota modulation
The synergy mechanism between AG and flavonoids. The AG matrix protects molecules from degradation in the small intestine; prebiotic effect on Lactobacillus enhances polyphenol absorption.
