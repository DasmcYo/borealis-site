Quality Control

How every batch gets verified

01
Raw material intake
Incoming larch wood is tested for species identity (PCR), moisture content, and absence of pesticide residues before entering extraction.
02
In-process monitoring
Extraction temperature, solvent concentration (water only), and yield ratios are logged continuously. HPLC spot-check at 50% and 100% of batch.
03
Final batch analysis
Full CoA issued: DHQ purity (HPLC), heavy metals (ICP-MS: Pb, Cd, Hg, As), microbiology (TPC, yeast, mould, E.coli, Salmonella), residual solvents.
04
Independent lab confirmation
Sample retained from every batch and sent to accredited 3rd-party lab. CoA from independent analysis provided with each shipment — not just our internal report.
