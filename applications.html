Where does Larifolin
fit your formulation?

Application 01
Nutraceuticals & Supplements
Vitamins, minerals, botanicals, collagen — any active with bioavailability challenges. Larifolin's AG matrix enhances the absorption of co-formulated actives while DHQ adds antioxidant protection and AG contributes prebiotic fibre value.
Explore →
Application 02
Sports Nutrition
Intense training generates reactive oxygen species that impair recovery. DHQ neutralises exercise-induced oxidative stress; AG supports the gut microbiome that underpins protein absorption and immune function in athletes.
Explore →
Application 03
Functional Food & Beverage
Stable to 150°C, water-soluble at ~1.5 g/L, neutral organoleptic profile, no emulsifiers required. Larifolin passes through food-processing conditions intact and qualifies as a clean-label functional ingredient.
Explore →
Application 04
Pharmaceuticals
GMP-certified, ≥99% HPLC purity, full documentation on every lot. DHQ is the only flavonoid with peer-reviewed confirmation of blood-brain barrier penetration (PNAS 2019), making it relevant to CNS and neuroprotective pharmaceutical development.
Explore →
Application 05
Longevity & Anti-aging
Inflammaging — chronic low-grade inflammation — is the primary molecular driver of accelerated aging. DHQ directly addresses inflammatory pathways; AG supports the gut-brain axis through prebiotic activity; together they address multiple hallmarks of cellular aging simultaneously.
Explore →
