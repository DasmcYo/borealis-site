The challenge:
bioavailability determines pharmacological relevance.

In drug-adjacent product development — nutraceutical pharmaceuticals, botanical drugs, dietary supplements intended for specific health conditions — the bottleneck is rarely the active molecule. It is the ability to deliver that molecule at therapeutically meaningful concentrations to the target site.

DHQ is a well-studied flavonoid with documented effects across neurological, cardiovascular, and metabolic pathways. Its limitation as a stand-alone ingredient is conventional bioavailability: absorbed, yes — but in quantities that often fall below effective thresholds in clinical studies.

The Biofolin
advantage.

Arabinogalactan acts as a biological carrier matrix that measurably enhances DHQ absorption. Independent evidence suggests a ×2.8 bioavailability improvement — a difference that can shift clinical outcomes from ambiguous to significant.

DHQ in Biofolin has been shown in preclinical models to cross the blood-brain barrier, positioning it as a candidate active for neuroprotective formulations. The 99% HPLC purity and documented production chain satisfy the baseline requirements for GMP-compatible ingredient sourcing in regulated pharmaceutical contexts.
